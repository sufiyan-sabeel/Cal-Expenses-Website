Features

Everything you need to stay on top of your money

Built as a focused, offline-first Android app — no account to create, no subscription, no ads.

Expense & income tracking

Record what you spend and earn with amount, category, title and date/time — and edit or delete any entry later.

Monthly budgets

Set a per-category limit and see how much you have spent, what is left and the percentage used — with a clear warning when you go over.

Savings goals

Create goals with a target amount and a target date, track percentage progress and remaining amount, and add money as you save.

Charts & insights

A six-month spending trend, a category breakdown donut and a weekly view, all calculated from your own stored transactions.

Search, filters & history

Find any transaction by text, filter by All / Expenses / Income / Transfers, and browse a history grouped by date.

Voice notes & dictation

Record audio locally and dictate text with your device's speech recognition. Typing always works as a fallback.

Tasks & reminders

Turn notes into tasks, add checklists and due dates, and get local reminder notifications on your device.

PDF reports & backups

Generate a print-quality multi-page PDF statement on-device, export expenses as CSV, and back up or restore everything as JSON.

Cal AI assistant Optional

Ask questions about your own figures in plain language. Uses OpenRouter's free models router and your own API key, stored encrypted on the device.

Also included: light and dark themes, AMOLED-friendly surfaces, and JSON backup import with validation.

About

A privacy-first personal finance app

Cal Expenses is an offline-first Android expense tracker created by Umaiz Sufiyan. Its mission is to make financial awareness accessible without subscriptions, accounts or ads.

Where your data lives

Transactions, budgets, goals, notes, tasks and settings are stored in a private database inside the app on your device. There is no Cal Expenses account and no Cal Expenses server — nothing is uploaded to us.

The one exception

The optional Cal AI assistant sends the message you type — and, only if you turn on the spending-summary switch, a short summary of your totals — to OpenRouter so a model can reply. Leave it unused and the app makes no network requests at all.

FAQ

Common questions

Is this app free?

Yes. The app is free to download and use, with no subscription, no ads and no paid service required for any core feature.

Why is it an APK and not on the Play Store?

Cal Expenses is distributed as a direct APK download. That keeps it free of store accounts and fees. It is signed with the Android debug key, so it is intended for sideloading rather than store distribution.

How do I install it?

Download the APK, open it on your Android device, and follow Android's prompts. Android will likely ask you to allow installs from the app you opened the file with. That is a standard Android protection — approve it for that app, install, and you can revoke the permission afterwards.

Which Android versions are supported?

Android 7.0 (API 24) or newer. It targets Android 14 (API 34).

Does it work without internet?

Yes. Expenses, budgets, goals, notes, tasks, charts and PDF reports are computed and stored entirely on your device and work with no connection. The only feature that needs the internet is the optional Cal AI assistant.

Does it collect my data?

The app contains no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and there is no Cal Expenses server to receive your financial data. The single exception is the optional Cal AI assistant, which sends your message to OpenRouter. See the privacy policy for the detail.

How do I update to a newer version?

Download the newer APK from this page and install it over the existing app. Your data is kept because the app's database is upgraded rather than replaced. Always export a JSON backup from Settings first if you want an extra safety net.

How do I back up or move my data?

Settings → Data & backup lets you export a JSON backup, export expenses as CSV, and import a JSON backup. Files are saved wherever you choose through Android's file picker.

How do I delete my data?

You can delete individual entries in the app, or uninstall Cal Expenses — uninstalling removes the app's local database and any recordings it stored.
